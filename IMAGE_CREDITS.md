## Restaurant photography

Restaurant-specific photos were removed from the restaurant cards because their reuse permissions and exact venue match could not be verified during this audit. The interface uses a neutral placeholder instead of presenting an unverified or potentially unlicensed photo as if it were the restaurant's own image. Replace these placeholders only with venue photos that have permission or a clearly reusable license.

## Hosting note

These photos are served from Wikimedia Commons' upload CDN, not bundled locally in the repository. If Wikimedia rate-limits image requests, download the credited files into `public/images/foods/` and update the paths while keeping the attribution.
